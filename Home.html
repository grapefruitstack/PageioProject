Welcome to the Storybook Playground

This is a simple storybook platforming project. You can explore stories like Alice in Wonderland by moving your avatar through the story's platforms.

Play the Game

This is a platform! Don't fall off!

How to Play

Keyboard: A / D: Move left and right. Space: Jump

Mouse or touch: Tap left, right, or top of screen to move.

Click: Move through links

Backspace / Back: Return to previous page

About This Project

This website is a small platforming project prototype. When complete, you'll be able to use your avatar to explore a variety of stories. Currently, only Chapter One of our version of Alice in Wonderland is ready to explore.
